Recently, I just got a cheap VPS with this specs:

- OS: Debian 13 (Trixie)
- RAM: 2 GB
- CPU: 2 Core

At first, I deployed Dokploy and managed Whatsapp Bot, a static Astro Website, [Mazanoke](https://github.com/civilblur/mazanoke), [Umami analytics](https://umami.is/), and [Forgejo](https://forgejo.org/). See the post in [Linkedin](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/haikel_bismillah-alhamdulillah-belakangan-ini-ugcPost-7460864471373168640-9_WH/?utm_source=social_share_send&utm_medium=member_desktop_web&rcm=ACoAADSMQ0kBxlAlYZ-yUckWTiO9tUY-rKwEc4I). But then I'm thinking to switch to k8s.

Is it overengineering? Actually yes. But as long as it's a personal VPS and for learning-purpose, why not.

For the kernel, I replace the Psycachy kernel with [Xanmod 7.0.14](https://xanmod.org/). After that, I'm messing around w/k8s in the VPS. I deployed 3 apps:
